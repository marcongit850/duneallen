Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Friends of Dune Allen respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it on behalf of Dune Allen Beach, Inc.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, message, and phone number if you choose to provide it. We also use Google Analytics, which uses cookies to tell us how many people visit and which pages they view. It does not tell us who you are.

How we use it

We use your information only to read and reply to your message. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Sharing

We never sell or rent your information. Messages are delivered through the companies that host and run this website (Cloudflare and Google Analytics), and we may share information if the law requires it.

Storage and security

Contact form messages are delivered by email and are not saved in a database on this website. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online system is completely secure.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To ask us to delete a message you sent, just reach out through the contact form.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
