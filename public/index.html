Since 1925

A Family’s Vision. A Community’s History.

The Allen family’s roots in South Walton reach back more than a century. E.C. Allen purchased the land in 1925 and in 1946, Dune Allen Beach, Inc. was formed, creating one of South Walton’s earliest beach communities that so many families cherish today.

Central to that history was an intention to set aside beach for community use, a place where generations could gather, enjoy the Gulf, and build lasting memories.

They helped create a community. Today, we’re working to preserve that legacy.

The dispute

What Is the Court Case About?

The dispute concerns ownership of the beach lying beyond the 315-foot boundary described in certain beachfront owners’ deeds, extending toward the waterline.

Those homeowners are seeking ownership of that additional beach, which Dune Allen Beach, Inc. maintains it owns. Dune Allen Beach, Inc. is defending its ownership and the intent reflected in the Allen family’s original deeds.

Beyond the 315-foot line

The case is about the beach past the boundary described in certain beachfront owners’ deeds, toward the waterline. Dune Allen Beach, Inc. maintains that it owns that beach.

The record so far

Where the Case Stands

In October 2024, the trial court granted summary judgment in favor of the homeowners. Dune Allen Beach, Inc. appealed, and the appellate court reversed that ruling and sent the case back for further proceedings.

That was an important step forward. The ownership dispute remains unresolved, with trial scheduled for January 2027.

The shoreline

Why This Matters to Our Community

This case concerns a stretch of beach woven into Dune Allen’s history and identity.

Dune Allen Beach, Inc.’s effort to defend its ownership is also an effort to preserve the founders’ intention that the beach be available for community enjoyment. The outcome matters to residents, visitors, and families whose connection to Dune Allen spans generations.

Protecting that legacy means standing up for the place (and the vision) that helped bring this community together.
